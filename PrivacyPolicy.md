## WarmSpot
I store the pictures, ratings, and locations of your uploads on my server. Only I have access to the server and I do not sell anything to anyone. No information uploaded is available to anything other than me and users of the app. 

There are purely optional ads. For these ads to work, third parties collect advertising data  --  Nothing else.


## PickaxePanic
I don't collect, store, or have access to any of your data. All game progress (upgrades, currencies, your save) stays on your device -- I never see it, and it's never sent to any server of mine.

There are purely optional ads. For these ads to work, third parties collect advertising data -- Nothing else. You may see a one-time prompt asking to allow tracking for these ads; you can say no and everything still works exactly the same.
